Hi! My name is Sophie Silver. I am a scientific researcher with a passion for the stories that data can tell about the universal behaviors of our world. I received my Doctorate in Earth & Environmental Science from the University of Pennsylvania where I developed and implemented a novel analytical framework to categorize planetary surfaces and ground-truthed that framework with laboratory experimentation. I also hold a Bachelors in Geoscience from Temple University.

While not in the lab, I served as the Vice President of Finance for the University of Pennsylvania's School of Arts & Sciences Graduate Student Government for 3 years. In this role, I managed the group's budget, delivered regular utilization reports, and awarded grants. 

In my free time, I enjoy bouldering and sport climbing, hiking, and live music.
